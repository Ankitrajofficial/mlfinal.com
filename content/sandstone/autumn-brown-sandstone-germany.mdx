## Stone at Source

Autumn Brown is one of our signature Vindhyan sandstone varieties, harvested directly from our Nayanagar quarry in the Bijolia belt. With a history spanning over half a billion years, this stone brings a timeless, organic warmth that is highly sought after for European garden design. As KHADANE™ is the quarry owner, processor, and exporter, we manage the entire supply chain to ensure the consistency that German importers require.

## Why Autumn Brown Suits German Landscapes

The German market values natural aesthetics that blend seamlessly with high-performance residential and public spaces. Autumn Brown is characterised by its distinctive palette of warm browns, buffs, and soft cinnamon tones. It provides a natural, rustic appearance while possessing the structural density required to withstand the cycle of freezing and thawing common in German winters. Whether used in classic courtyard designs or contemporary patio layouts, its characterful surface ensures that no two stones are identical, providing a bespoke look for every project.

## Precision Specifications

We process our sandstone in our own units to meet specific technical requirements. For the German trade, we focus on calibrated thicknesses and clean edge profiles.

### Available Formats
- **Pavings:** We supply standard formats including 900x600, 600x600, and 600x290 mm. We also provide patio packs for mixed-size patterns and French pattern sets for larger, irregular layouts.
- **Stepping Stones & Circles:** Available in various diameters and shapes to add focal points to landscape design.
- **Copings & Edging:** Machine-cut or bullnosed options to ensure a refined finish for pool decks, garden walls, and steps.
- **Wall Cladding:** Available in Z-panel formats or traditional loose-stone styles for natural vertical surfacing.

### Surface and Edge Finishes
- **Natural Riven:** The classic finish showcasing the stone's inherent geological texture.
- **Tumbled:** Softened edges and faces, ideal for a weathered, mature aesthetic.
- **Sawn & Honed:** For projects requiring a modern, uniform, and slip-resistant surface.
- **Edge Profiles:** We provide hand-cut edges for a traditional look, or straight machine-cut edges for a precise, modern installation.
