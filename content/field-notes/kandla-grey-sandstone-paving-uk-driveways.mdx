## Why Kandla Grey suits driveway projects

Driveways ask more of a paving stone than a patio does. There is vehicle weight, repeated wear at tyre tracks, and in the UK, long stretches of wet weather and frost. Kandla Grey is one of the stones we quarry most for exactly this kind of specification. It is a silver grey Vindhyan sandstone with a soft riven figure, cut from our own blocks in the Bijolia belt, and it has become the export volume stone in our range for good reason: consistent colour, dependable hardness, and a neutral tone that suits both modern and traditional house fronts.

As quarry owners rather than traders, we select the blocks ourselves before they are ever cut, which is part of why buyers come back to Kandla Grey for larger driveway orders where colour matching across a full job matters.

### Sizes and formats for driveway work

For domestic and trade driveway projects, Kandla Grey is most often specified as a paving (KHF-001). Standard sizes include:

- 900x600, 600x600, 600x290 and 290x290 mm
- Larger formats: 600x900 and 600x1200 mm
- Mixed-size patio packs: 66 pieces (19.61 m2) or 48 pieces (14.72 m2)

Where a driveway needs a harder wearing edge detail, cobble setts (KHF-003) in Kandla Grey are also common, either in UK sizes (200x100, 100x100, 200x50 mm) or continental sizes (140x140, 200x140 mm), often used to border a paved area or to pick out a turning circle.

Any of these formats can also be cut to a buyer's own specification where a project calls for a non-standard size.

### Thickness: what driveways actually need

Driveway paving generally wants to be thicker than patio paving, simply because of vehicle load. Our standard calibrated range runs 18, 20, 22, 25 and 30 mm, with 22 mm as the standard thickness across the paving format. For a domestic driveway taking regular car weight, 30 mm calibrated is the thickness we recommend trade buyers specify, laid on an appropriate sub-base. Setts, by comparison, run thicker again at 40 to 60 mm (nominal 50 mm), which is part of why they are often used at the edges and entry points that take the most direct wheel contact.

All calibrated thicknesses are supplied to +/-2 mm, so a full driveway order lays flat and consistent without extensive packing on site.

### Finish and edge choices

Kandla Grey is available across our full surface range, but for driveways two finishes dominate UK specification:

- **Natural riven**: the standing production finish, with the soft riven figure that gives Kandla Grey its texture and grip underfoot and under tyre.
- **Sawn or honed**: a flatter, more contemporary face, worked to order, popular where a driveway sits alongside a modern extension or render finish.

Edge detail should follow the face finish. Riven paving takes a hand cut edge (18 to 30 mm stone), which follows the natural break of the stone with a true top arris. Sawn paving takes a machine cut edge, square to +/-2 mm with no tooling. The two are not interchangeable: a sawn face does not sit well with a hand cut edge, and vice versa. If a driveway design calls for a bullnose detail at a step or threshold, that is worked on sawn stone only.

### Ordering for a UK driveway project

Because Kandla Grey is natural stone, colour and markings vary piece to piece, and we always flag this when sending photos or samples so a buyer knows what to expect in a full pallet versus a single slab. For larger driveway orders, we recommend ordering from a single approved sample lot to keep colour consistent across the whole area, which is straightforward for us to manage since we are cutting from our own quarry blocks rather than buying in mixed parcels.

We ship FOB Mundra, and for driveway projects we would need to know the paving or sett format, size, thickness, finish and edge, the quantity required, and the destination port before preparing a formal quotation. Lead time is normally 2 to 3 weeks from confirmed order to FOB Mundra, with regular production items sometimes ready in 8 to 10 days.
