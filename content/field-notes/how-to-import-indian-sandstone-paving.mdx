## Understanding the Direct Supply Chain

For many European landscapers, importers, and stone merchants, the transition from local wholesale to direct sourcing can significantly improve margins and batch consistency. When you import Indian sandstone paving, the key to success lies in understanding the difference between a trader and a quarry owner. At KHADANE™, we have been working with Vindhyan sandstone for over five decades. By operating our own processing units at the source in Bijolia, we ensure that every square metre is graded and packed according to specific export requirements.

### Why Source Directly from the Quarry?

Indian sandstone is a natural material, formed over half a billion years. Because it is a sedimentary stone, geological variations are inevitable. When you source through multiple middlemen, the original batch consistency is often lost as stone is consolidated from various small sites. As quarry owners, we control the extraction from our 100+ quarries and manage the processing in-house. This allows us to provide more uniform batches of popular varieties like Kandla Grey, Autumn Brown, and Raj Blend, as the quality control begins at the pit face rather than at the port.

### Defining Your Specification

Before you initiate an order, clear technical specifications are essential. Sandstone is available in a variety of thicknesses, with 20mm and 22mm calibrated paving being standard for European markets. You must decide on the surface finish, natural riven, honed, or tumbled, and the edge profile. 

*   **Natural Riven:** The classic look, following the stone's natural bedding planes.
*   **Sawn and Honed:** Provides a contemporary, uniform finish suitable for modern patio designs.
*   **Edges:** Hand-cut edges offer a traditional aesthetic, while machine-cut edges provide the precision required for tight-joint contemporary paving layouts.

### The Logistics of FOB Mundra

In the stone export industry, the standard commercial arrangement is FOB Mundra (Free on Board). This means the supplier is responsible for the transport of the stone to the Mundra port and the loading costs, while the buyer assumes control of the shipment from the moment it is on board the vessel. 

We provide all formal quotations on an FOB basis to ensure transparency. If you are new to the import process, we can also assist with special arrangements for CIF (Cost, Insurance, and Freight) to your designated port. Regardless of the incoterm, clear documentation, including the Bill of Lading, packing lists, and quality certificates, is prepared by our accounts team to ensure a seamless transit through customs at your destination.

### Quality Assurance and Packaging

Transporting heavy natural stone requires professional packing. All our export shipments are packed in fumigated wooden crates. For heavy items like block steps or large format slabs, we use specialized bearers to prevent breakage during transit. When you receive your shipment, the consistency of the stone should match the samples previously approved. If you are looking to secure a consistent supply for a large landscaping project, we recommend requesting a sample set from our current production run to verify the colour range and finish.
