## Understanding Indian Sandstone Finishes

When importing natural stone, the surface finish dictates both the aesthetic appeal and the functional performance of the material. At KHADANE™, we process Vindhyan sandstone, a stone over half a billion years old, using specific surface technologies to meet the diverse requirements of the European and international markets. As quarry owners, we maintain control over the finish from the moment the block is extracted to the final cut in our processing units.

### Natural Riven: The Authentic Texture

The most traditional finish for Indian sandstone is the natural riven surface. This texture is achieved by splitting the stone along its natural bedding planes. Because it is a split face, every piece is unique, showcasing the inherent character and geological movement of the Vindhyan strata. 

Natural riven stone is ideal for traditional landscaping projects where grip and rustic character are essential. It is typically paired with hand-cut edges, which complement the uneven surface profile. While riven stone is visually striking, it requires a larger tolerance in thickness compared to machine-cut options.

### Sawn and Honed: Contemporary Precision

For modern architectural designs, a uniform, level surface is often required. Sawn finishes are created using precision saws, resulting in a square edge and a flat, smooth surface. When we take the sawn face and process it further with abrasive heads, we produce a honed finish. 

These surfaces are favoured for high-traffic pedestrian areas and interior applications. Because the stone is calibrated to a consistent thickness, commonly 20mm or 22mm, it offers faster installation for contractors. Unlike riven stone, honed surfaces allow for tighter joint lines and a cleaner, more minimalist aesthetic.

### Advanced Textures: Sandblasted and Flamed

Beyond basic surface treatments, advanced finishes can alter both the colour and the safety profile of the stone:

*   **Sandblasted:** High-pressure sand is projected onto the surface, removing the top layer to create a consistent, matte finish. This is highly effective for increasing the slip resistance of lighter stones like Raveena or Mint.
*   **Flamed:** Using high-temperature torches, we treat the stone surface to cause thermal shock, creating a rough, textured finish. This is particularly popular for darker stones like Slate Grey, where it enhances the deep, uniform colour of the material.
*   **Tumbled:** To give stone an aged, weathered appearance, we place tiles into tumbling machines. This softens the sharp edges and creates a slightly worn surface, mimicking stone that has been exposed to the elements for years.

### Selecting the Right Finish for Your Market

As a trade buyer, selecting the right finish should be determined by the project environment and the stone variety chosen. For example, Kandla Grey is extremely versatile and works well in both natural riven and sandblasted finishes. Conversely, denser materials like Basalt Black (a volcanic stone, not a sandstone) are often supplied with machine-cut, sawn, or flamed surfaces to highlight their dark, modern character.
