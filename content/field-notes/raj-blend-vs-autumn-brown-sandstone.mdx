## Raj Blend vs Autumn Brown sandstone: choosing a warm paving stone

Buyers building a warm-toned paving range often narrow their shortlist to two KHADANE™ varieties: Raj Blend and Autumn Brown. Both read as warm sandstones from a distance, but they come from different beds, carry different movement, and suit different ranges. This note sets out the practical differences so you can specify the right one, or stock both for different customers.

### What Raj Blend looks like

Raj Blend has a green-grey ground with plum and buff movement running through it. It is sometimes known in the trade as Raj Blend, but we always call it Raj Blend, since the finished slab reads as a blend of tones rather than a single green. On a patio or driveway, Raj Blend sits closer to the cooler end of the warm spectrum. It works well where a buyer wants a stone that is not plain grey but also not a strong brown or terracotta.

The movement within a single slab can be considerable. Plum and buff streaks appear against the green-grey base, so no two pieces are identical. This is natural variation, not a defect, and it is worth setting that expectation with customers when you send photographs or samples.

### What Autumn Brown looks like

Autumn Brown is a more straightforward warm stone: browns and buffs across the face, with the kind of tonal range the name suggests. It is quarried at our Nayanagar quarry within the Bijolia belt. Where Raj Blend carries a cooler undertone, Autumn Brown leans fully into warm autumnal colour, making it a natural match for brick, timber cladding or terracotta planting schemes.

Autumn Brown tends to photograph and sell well in ranges positioned around traditional or cottage-garden landscaping, where buyers want a stone that complements red brick and older property stock. Raj Blend suits more contemporary or mixed-material schemes where a muted, blended tone works better than strong brown.

### Formats and sizes

Both varieties are owned stones, quarried directly by KHADANE, and both are available across the full format range. In practice, most export orders for these two varieties fall into a few common categories:

- **Pavings** (KHF-001): standard sizes 900x600, 600x600, 600x290 and 290x290 mm, with mixed-size patio packs available. Thickness runs 18 to 30 mm, with 22 mm calibrated as the standard.
- **Flagstones / crazy paving** (KHF-002): irregular pieces specified by pieces per square metre, useful where a buyer wants a more informal, reclaimed look in either colourway.
- **Stepping stones** (KHF-004): rectangular or round formats, useful for garden paths that need to tie into a larger patio laid in the same stone.
- **Copings and kerbstones**: both varieties can be finished to the edge profiles required for walls, pools or driveway borders.

As with all KHADANE varieties, any format can also be cut to a buyer's own specification, so if your range calls for a size outside the standard list, it is worth raising it directly with our team.

### Finish options

Natural riven and tumbled are standing production finishes for both Raj Blend and Autumn Brown, meaning they are readily available without a long lead-in. Sawn, honed, sandblast, flamed and the other finishes in our surfaces range are worked to order for either stone.

Finish choice affects how the colour reads. A riven Raj Blend shows more depth and shadow in the plum and buff streaking, since the broken surface catches light unevenly. A honed or sawn finish on the same stone flattens that effect slightly, giving a smoother, more uniform colour block, which some contemporary ranges prefer. The same logic applies to Autumn Brown: riven suits a rustic or traditional presentation, while sawn and honed suit a cleaner, more architectural look.

### Which to choose

If your customers are matching to red brick, older stonework or a traditional garden style, Autumn Brown is usually the more direct fit. If you need a warm stone that also works alongside grey or render, or want something with more tonal complexity for a feature area, Raj Blend tends to perform better.

Many of our trade buyers stock both, using Autumn Brown as the dominant warm line and Raj Blend as a complementary or premium option within the same range. Since both are owned-quarry stones from the Bijolia belt, lead times and quality control follow the same process at source, which makes it straightforward to run them side by side in a catalogue.
